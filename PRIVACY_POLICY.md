# Privacy Policy for Device Manager for Alexa

**Effective Date:** September 2026

Thank you for using "Device Manager for Alexa". Your privacy is extremely important to us. This policy outlines how your data is handled.

## 1. Local Processing Only
This extension operates **100% locally** within your browser. 
We do **not** use any external servers, databases, or third-party analytics to process your data. 

## 2. No Data Collection
We do **not** collect, store, share, or sell any personal information, device data, Amazon account details, passwords, or browsing history.

## 3. How the Extension Works
To function, the extension requires access to the Amazon Alexa web interface (e.g., lexa.amazon.com, lexa.amazon.de). 
It only communicates directly with Amazon's official endpoints on your behalf, using your already active and authenticated browser session. No data ever leaves your computer except to talk directly to Amazon.

## 4. Permissions
The extension requests the following permissions:
- **Host Permissions (Amazon Domains):** Required strictly to fetch your smart home device list and send disable/delete commands to Amazon's servers at your request.
- **ActiveTab & Scripting:** Required to inject the manager user interface over the Alexa web page.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
